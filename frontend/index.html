How to Use

  1. Upload Waste Image
    Choose or drag an image of the waste item you want to classify.
  2. Click Analyze Image
    Press the “Analyse Image” button and wait for the AI to process the image.
  3. View Classification Result
    TRASHKUYYY will show the waste category, confidence score, description, recycling method, and environmental impact.
  4. Learn How to Manage It
    Read the recommended waste management steps to understand how to dispose of or recycle the item properly.
  5. Check Your History
    Open the History page to see your previous classification results.

TRASHKUYY is an AI-powered waste classification website designed to help users identify different types of waste quickly and easily. Through technology, we want to encourage people to better understand their waste and take small actions that support a cleaner and more sustainable environment.
